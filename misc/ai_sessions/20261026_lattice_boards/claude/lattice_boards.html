How big each FPGA is

Logic cells, largest first. An iCE40 logic cell is one 4-input LUT with a flip-flop; Lattice counts the ECP5 and MachXO2 in 4-input LUTs too, so the numbers compare directly. Bars share one scale, up to the ECP5-85F's 84K.

OrangeCrab r0.2.1

ECP5
Identified by
Orange Feather-size PCB with the “GsD” logo of its designer, Greg Davill. The USB-C port dates it: the maker's page says r0.2.1 switched from micro-USB to USB-C.
FPGA
ECP5 LFE5U-25F or LFE5U-85F in the csfBGA285 package: 24K or 84K LUTs
On board
The square chip with the QR label is the ECP5; the tall chip to its right is DDR3L memory, 128 MB or 512 MB. 128 Mbit QSPI flash, USB-C wired straight to the FPGA, a LiPo battery connector and one button.
Programming
DFU bootloader over USB-C, with dfu-util
BGM
Supportedorangecrab_ecp5_yosys: 48 MHz clock, built for the 25F in the CSFBGA285 package with Yosys and nextpnr
Sources
  • OrangeCrab r0.2.1 hardware page
  • Getting started (DFU upload)
  • Lattice ECP5 family datasheet (LUT counts)

UnsureWhether this is the 25F or the 85F. The part number is under the QR label.

MuseLab iCESugar-nano v1.2

iCE40
Identified by
Silkscreen “iCESugar-nano” and “v1.2”
FPGA
iCE40LP1K-CM36, 1,280 logic cells, a 2.5 mm BGA. It is the small square chip near the pin end, not the larger one beside the USB-C, which is the iCELink microcontroller (APM32F1).
On board
USB-C, 2 MB SPI flash, one yellow LED, one 2 × 6 Pmod at the far end and a 1 × 6 Pmod down each side
Programming
iCELink: copy the bitstream onto the USB drive it presents, or use icesprog. The same cable gives a USB serial port and a clock of 8, 12, 36 or 72 MHz for the FPGA (12 MHz after power-up).
BGM
Not yetNo board folder

24-bit DVI Pmod

Add-on
Identified by
Silkscreen “iCEBreaker PMOD” and “24bit DVI”. 1BitSquared sells 4-bit, 12-bit and 24-bit DDR versions.
Adds
DVI video on an HDMI connector, through a TI TFP410 or its drop-in replacement, the SiI164. The chip marking in the photo can't tell which.
Connects
Double Pmod on PMOD1A and PMOD1B, with pins D0–D11, CK, DE, HS and VS. Twelve data lines carry 24-bit colour by sending 12 bits on the rising and 12 on the falling clock edge.

iCE40-IO and basics-graphics-music

Olimex's netlists for the iCE40HX8K-EVB Rev.B and the iCE40-IO Rev.A give each pin of the 34-pin bus. Pins 1–4 match on both boards (+5V, GND, +3V3, GND), so the numbers line up. The VGA pins in ice40hx8k_evb_yosys/board_specific.pcf don't follow the iCE40-IO: its sync signals land on the PS/2 pins and its red bits on the iCE40-IO's sync pins. With this iCE40-IO plugged in, that folder can't show a correct picture. Either it was written for a different VGA adapter, or it needs a variant for the iCE40-IO.
