Found while checking sources

Not a report error, but a problem in the repo

F1ice40hx8k_evb_yosys doesn't fit the iCE40-IO in your photoyour call

Olimex's netlists give every pin of the 34-pin bus on both boards, and pins 1–4 (+5V, GND, +3V3, GND) line up. The folder's VGA pins don't follow the iCE40-IO: HSYNC and VSYNC land on its PS/2 pins, the red bits on its sync pins, and blue bit 1 on its IrDA transmitter. The iCE40-IO also has only 3 bits per colour, against the folder's 4. Its UART pins, 10 and 12, are the iCE40-IO's IrDA lines.

pin 14: iCE40-IO PS2_CLK · BGM VGA_HS
pin 16: iCE40-IO PS2_DATA · BGM VGA_VS
pin 18: iCE40-IO VGA_VSYNC · BGM VGA_R[0]
pin 20: iCE40-IO VGA_HSYNC · BGM VGA_R[1]
pin 34: iCE40-IO VGA_RED3 · BGM VGA_B[0]

Either the folder was written for a different VGA adapter, or it needs an iCE40-IO variant. The full pin table is in my board page. I haven't changed the repo.
