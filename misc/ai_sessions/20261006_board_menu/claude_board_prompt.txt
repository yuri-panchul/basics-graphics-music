Note that de0, de0_nano_soc and de0_nano are different boards and should not be
confused as variants. Same thing about de1 and de1_soc, or de2 and de2_115.
Or omdazz and omdazz_epm570.

Generally you can figure out which board is a variant by examining .qsf, .xdc, .lpf or .pcf file.
However it should be done carefully because some variants of the same board
comment out different pins in .cst file. In addition, some boards, like
gowin boards have variants for Gowin EDA or Yosys toolchains.

So creating a menu based purely on suffixes should be sufficient. You most
likely don't need to check board-specific files before giving user the menu.

However I want you to check the the final two-level selection process is
equivalent to the original one.
