Where Codex's sources didn't hold up

  • Caltech EE/CS/MedE 125: its page now returns 404 and the course isn't in the 2026–27 catalog. Codex itself noted it wasn't offered in 2025–26. EE/CS 119, which is offered in 2026–27, is the better pick.
  • MIET's Verilog practicum syllabus: the PDF Codex linked now returns 404.
  • KSE: Codex rated the whole master's program. The curriculum names a specific required course, VLSI 1: HDL Based Design for FPGAs.
  • Unreachable from here, so not checked: the MSU physics page, the BNTU manual mirror, the KRSU Tempus report, the VILNIUS TECH article, the Agricultural University of Georgia presentation and Nazarbayev's ECE 482 page (expired certificate).

Strengths and gaps

Codex's report

Strengths

  • Strong evidence discipline: every entry has a dated source and separates what students do from how it compares.
  • Picked up good courses I'd missed: Stanford EE 109, UCLA 152B and the BSUIR lab course.
  • Useful as a tool: filters, search and print, with no external fonts or scripts.
  • Rendered and screenshot-checked in a browser.

Gaps

  • Less than a third of the universities; five republics have no entry at all.
  • Missed the strongest open Moscow materials: MIET's APS course, MIPT's Verilog course and MIEM's SoC course.
  • Rates some lab-only courses as close (KPI, TalTech, RTU, Nazarbayev).
  • Some sources were already stale on the day (Caltech 125, MIET's PDF).
  • Covers the Synthesis School only briefly.

This report

Strengths

  • Broader: every School cluster university, the Bay Area schools and Harvey Mudd, and 2 to 4 universities in each republic.
  • Chooses specific courses (KSE VLSI 1, MIET APS, MIPT, MIEM) instead of programs.
  • Full Synthesis School section, local-language names everywhere, and a regional summary.

Gaps

  • 14 rows still rest on search results because their pages didn't load from here.
  • No filters or search, and no evidence date on each row.
  • Loads fonts from Google Fonts, and hasn't been checked in a browser.

Still open

  • BNTU: Codex cites a 2013 VHDL lab manual through an Uzbek library mirror. If it checks out, BNTU becomes Partial.
  • MSU physics: Codex describes 48 contact hours each semester and a project defense. The page has to load before the row can be marked checked.
  • Nazarbayev ECE 482: if the page confirms the FPGA accelerator project, Nazarbayev becomes Close.
  • The rating disagreements (KPI, TalTech, RTU, MIPT, MPEI, MIEM) come down to whether a lab sequence without a large project of the student's own design counts as close. This report says it doesn't.

Method

I read Codex's English page and its course_data.json, fetched its 38 course source links with curl, and tried WebFetch on several that failed. Its claims were adopted only where the source loaded and said what Codex reported. Counts for this report are computed from its generator data, so they match the report pages.
