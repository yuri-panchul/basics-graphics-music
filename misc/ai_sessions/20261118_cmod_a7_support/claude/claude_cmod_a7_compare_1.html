Verdict

Codex's RTL is sound. Its four configurations simulate correctly, all four build in Vivado, and the TinyVGA bit order, the sync polarities, the reset sequence and the I²S formats check out. We found no functional bug in its HDL; its 100 MHz clock leaves the heaviest lab we tried only +0.693 ns of slack.

The problems lie around the HDL: a 5 V rail on the breadboard next to the FPGA pins, a separate-supply option that can feed 3.3 V into an unpowered FPGA, a catalog row that breaks the catalog's conventions, “Generated by Codex” banners on shared files Codex only edited, and three configurations without HTML or a Russian guide.

Codex is ahead on verification. Its self-checking testbench, its check that the XDC only uncomments Digilent's lines, and its Vivado script with structural assertions are worth taking as they are. Eight GPIO pins, a reset/run LED and TinyVGA files pinned to one revision are small features worth taking too.

Where Codex is ahead

  • Automated tests in the repository: simulation, lab elaboration, XDC provenance
  • Eight lab GPIO pins on DIP 1–8
  • RGB LED shows reset and run
  • TinyVGA production files pinned to a commit, with SHA-256

Where ours is ahead

  • Only 3.3 V on the breadboard; the 0.55 V unpowered limit documented
  • Every configuration has README.md, README_ru.md and HTML
  • 50 MHz clock: the spectrum analyzer keeps 9.3 ns of slack, against 0.7 ns
  • A 304-pixel no-PLL mode closer to standard VGA timing, and an even width
  • The catalog and the shared scripts keep the repository's conventions

Problems in Codex's branch

Ranked by severity. Paths are relative to boards/ of Codex's branch unless they start with scripts/.

Medium

A 5 V rail runs along 24 FPGA pins

cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_codex/assembly_en.md:138–153, breadboard_dac_en.svg, breadboard_dac_ru.svg

The guide makes the left power rail VU, the USB 5 V, and runs it the full length of the breadboard beside DIP 1–24 to feed the DAC and the 3.3 V regulator. The DIP pins have no series resistors, and Table 7.1 of Digilent's manual allows them 3.75 V at most while powered. A jumper pushed into the rail one hole off puts 5 V on an FPGA pin.

Fix: make both rails 3.3 V and keep 5 V to the single wire from DIP 24 to the regulator input. Both DAC boards run from 3.3 V: WeAct's ME6231 regulator gives about 3.2 V from it, within the 3.0–3.6 V of PCM5100A, and GY-PCM5102 takes 3.3–5 V on VIN. Our guide does this.

Medium

The separate-supply option can feed 3.3 V into an unpowered FPGA

cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_codex/assembly_en.md:58 and its Russian copy

If the rails sag, the guide suggests a separate accessory supply, with only the grounds shared. Unplug USB while that supply is on, and the pull-ups of the TM1638 module hold DIP 26–28 at 3.3 V against an unpowered FPGA. Table 7.1 allows 0.55 V on an unpowered DIP pin. The guide never states that limit: neither “0.55” nor “unpowered” occurs in it.

Fix: state the limit, and switch the accessory supply only together with the board, or drop the option. Our guide states it in its power section.

Low

100 MHz leaves the heaviest lab under 1 ns of slack

cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_codex/board_specific_top.sv:65–69

Codex clocks the whole design at 100 MHz to give vga.sv its 25 MHz pixel rate as a ÷4 enable. The heaviest lab we tried, labs/3_music/3_10_spectrum_analyzer, still meets timing, but with +0.693 ns of worst setup slack, against +9.347 ns on our 50 MHz clock. Its worst path is a 12-level carry chain in the spectrum filters. Not a bug today, but a student who adds a little logic to that lab will miss timing on Codex's configuration long before ours.

Fix: 50 MHz (12 × 62.5 ÷ 15, a 750 MHz VCO), the clock most boards in the repository run at, gives 640×480 with a ÷2 enable and doubles every lab's clock period.

Low

The catalog row breaks the catalog's conventions

README.csv:48, README.md:5, 57, 81, scripts/tests/test_board_menu.py:30

The row names a configuration, cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_codex, where every other row names a board. Three of its values occur nowhere else in their columns: “Vivado (Artix-7 device support)”, where the 13 other Xilinx rows say “Vivado, any version †”; “optional”, where the TM1638 column says always, never or either; and “TinyVGA Pmod”, where the graphics column says VGA, HDMI/DVI, LCD or a combination. The README still says “55 boards” and “23 of 55” over 56 rows. The board-menu test needed removesuffix("_codex").removesuffix("_tiny_vga") to map the row back to cmod_a7_35.

Fix: one row cmod_a7_35 with the usual values, the counts set to 56, and the test unchanged.

Low

“Generated by Codex” banners on files Codex only edited

line 1 of README.md, scripts/steps/00_setup.source_bash and scripts/tests/test_board_menu.py

Codex changed a few lines in each of these shared files, then headed the whole file “Generated by Codex GPT-6-Astra with xHigh efforts.” That misattributes code written by others, and every later AI-assisted edit would add its own banner.

Fix: credit in the commit message, or in a comment next to the changed lines.

Low

Three of four configurations have no HTML and no Russian guide

cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_no_pll_codex/, cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_no_tm1638_codex/, cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_no_pll_no_tm1638_codex/

The task asked for .md and HTML instructions, in English and in Russian. Each of these directories has a single English README.md with a short “Кратко по-русски” summary; the full instructions for them are sections of the main configuration's guide.

Fix: either a full guide pair per configuration, or a clear statement that the main guide covers all four.

Low

The RGB LED glows green at full brightness the whole time

cmod_a7_35_tiny_vga_codex/board_specific_top.sv:131–134

led0_g = rst holds the green cathode low, so the LED is on at 100 % duty whenever the design runs. Section 6.1 of Digilent's manual:

Digilent strongly recommends the use of pulse-width modulation (PWM) when driving the tri-color LED. Driving any of the inputs to a steady logic ‘1’ will result in the LED being illuminated at an uncomfortably bright level. You can avoid this by ensuring that none of the tri-color signals are driven with more than 50% duty cycle.

(The manual says ‘1’, although the LED lights on a 0.)

Fix: gate both colours with a 1/16 duty pulse from a free-running counter.

Nits

Small things

  • scripts/steps/00_setup.source_bash:624–627 names the four configurations one by one for the Vivado toolchain, with broken alignment. The glob cmod_a7_35* covers them and every later configuration.
  • The prompt appendix has no times. It includes prompts that have nothing to do with the board: P01 restore the session, P02 an action plan, P05 the Vivado licence, P07–P11 line endings.
  • The no-PLL width 305 is odd, so a lab that splits the screen in halves or tiles gets an uneven split. 304 with the same porches gives 381 clocks a line, 31.50 kHz and 59.99 Hz, nearer the 59.94 Hz standard than 305's 59.84 Hz.
  • With TM1638, BTN1 is ORed into key[0]. It duplicates S1 rather than adding a ninth key.

Datasheet checks

  • INMP441 needs SCK of 0.5–3.2 MHz and WS of 7.8–50 kHz. At 12 MHz both branches give 0.75 MHz and 11.7 kHz; at 50 and 100 MHz, 3.125 MHz and 48.8 kHz. All within range.
  • The PCM5100A and PCM5102A make their master clock from BCK at 64 BCK per frame: 3 MHz and 46.9 kHz at 12 MHz, 3.125 MHz and 48.8 kHz otherwise. Both fine.
  • Digilent's DIP drawing puts pin 1 at the Pmod end and VU and GND, pins 24 and 25, at the USB end. Codex's breadboard drawing agrees.

Gaps in ours, seen in the comparison

  • No tests in the repository, no GPIO, an unused RGB LED and unpinned TinyVGA files: the ideas above.
  • boards/README.csv says TM1638 “always” for cmod_a7_35, although three of our six configurations have none. The Tang boards with _no_tm1638 configurations also say “always”, and icebreaker says “either”, so the column's rule is unclear: which do you want?
  • Shared with Codex: no reset button without TM1638, and 25.0 MHz pixels instead of 25.175 MHz at 640×480.

Merging both

Both branches edit boards/README.csv, boards/README.md, boards/README.html, scripts/steps/00_setup.source_bash and scripts/tests/test_board_menu.py. Whichever is merged second will conflict in all five files. The board directories themselves have different names and do not collide.
