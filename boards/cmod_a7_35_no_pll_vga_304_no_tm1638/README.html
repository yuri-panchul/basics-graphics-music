Cmod A7-35T without TM1638 and without an MMCM: VGA 304x480

Board configuration cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304_no_tm1638. Русская версия: README_ru.md.

This is cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304 without the TM1638 module. The labs get the two buttons of Cmod A7 as their keys and its two LEDs as their LEDs, and there is no seven-segment display. Everything else is that of cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304: the 12 MHz clock without an MMCM, VGA 304x480 at the standard line and frame rates, the microphone, the DAC, the pins and the wiring. The other configurations without TM1638 are cmod_a7_35_no_tm1638, cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_320_no_tm1638.

What is different

cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304_no_tm1638, this one
Keys of the labs8 keys of TM1638BTN0 and BTN1 of Cmod A7: key[0] and key[1], also sw[0] and sw[1]
LEDs of the labs8 LEDs of TM1638, the first two also on LED1 and LED2 of Cmod A7LED1 and LED2 of Cmod A7: led[0] and led[1]
Seven-segment display8 digits of TM1638None
Reset buttonEither Cmod buttonNone: both buttons are keys
DIP 26, 27, 28TM1638 STB, CLK, DIOUnconnected

A button gives 1 when pressed; an LED lights on 1. The labs get one seven-segment digit that goes nowhere, so a lab that drives a display builds and runs, and its display output is lost. Whatever a lab shows on LEDs beyond the first two is lost the same way.

The design is in reset for the first three clock cycles after loading. No button resets it: to start a lab again, load the FPGA again with ./04_configure_fpga.bash.

The design keeps DIP 26 and 27, the STB and CLK of TM1638 in cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304, high, and does not drive DIP 28. Leave the three pins unconnected.

How it is made

board_specific_top.sv has two lines of code. It defines FORCE_NO_INSTANTIATE_TM1638_BOARD_CONTROLLER_MODULE and includes the board_specific_top.sv of cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304, which then leaves out TM1638 even though peripherals/lab_specific_board_config.svh turns it on. Other boards of the repository without TM1638, such as tang_nano_9k_hdmi_no_tm1638, are made the same way, but some of them draw a virtual TM1638 on the screen; this one does not.

board_specific.xdc is the one of cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304. It keeps the lines of DIP 26, 27 and 28 uncommented, because the included top level still has these ports.

The hardware and the wiring

The parts, the 3.3 V source, the assembly and the wiring tables are those of cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304 without TM1638: leave out the module and its five wires. DIP 25 still goes to the GND rail. The pins in short:

The drawings of this directory have no TM1638, for both DAC modules, WeAct I2S Earphone Module (PCM5100A) and GY-PCM5102 (PCM5102A).

Building and running the labs

In a lab directory, choose the board once with ./06_choose_another_fpga_board.bash and pick cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304_no_tm1638. Then build and load as for cmod_a7_35_no_pll_vga_304:

./03_synthesize_for_fpga.bash
./04_configure_fpga.bash

The scripts use Vivado for every board whose name starts with cmod_a7, so this configuration needs no change of the scripts.

Status

Built with Vivado 2026.1 with the configuration file of the scripts, peripherals/lab_specific_board_config.svh, which turns TM1638 on: the builds show that this configuration leaves it out all the same.

All builds have no errors and no critical warnings, and meet all timing at 12 MHz, a clock period of 83.3 ns, with no MMCM or PLL. None of the netlists has a TM1638 module. Vivado gives the design rule warning CFGBVS-1, as for cmod_a7_35; it does not stop the bitstream.

Not yet tried on a real board.
